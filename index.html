What's what

Unified smooth skin fuses every duct, ampulla, crus, utricle and saccule into one continuous surface, so you can see the membranous labyrinth as a single connected, fluid-filled system.

Fluid envelope (T2 MRI) is the whole fluid-filled inner ear as seen on MRI. Shown faintly, it stands in for the bony labyrinth that houses the membranous one, and it includes the cochlea.

Geometry: IE-Map in-vivo inner-ear atlas. Ahmadi S-A, Raiser TM, Rühl RM, Flanagin VL, zu Eulenburg P (2021). IE-Map: a novel in-vivo atlas and template of the human inner ear. Scientific Reports 11, 3293. doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-82716-0
